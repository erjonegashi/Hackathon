About PowerPulse

A compelling prototype for smarter home energy behavior.

PowerPulse is designed to make energy consumption intuitive and user-friendly. The app uses appliance-level tracking, cost estimates, and simulated peak events to help users spot waste, reduce load, and earn progress-based rewards. It is a strong fit for energy demo experiences, consumer testing, and hackathon presentations.

Features

Built to showcase energy efficiency with clarity and engagement.

Appliance-level visibility

Track each device’s wattage and active time so users know exactly what is using power in real time.

Usage-driven insights

Daily load totals, estimated cost, and top-consuming appliances are derived from live simulation data.

Peak event simulation

Challenge users to reduce their load during simulated peak windows, making energy savings feel actionable.

Reward mechanics

Points, streaks, and badges motivate ongoing engagement while reinforcing good energy habits.

Modern mobile-first UI

Designed with a polished dark interface, clear typography, and accessible layout for fast interaction.

Persistent experience

State is stored locally, so progress and appliance settings remain available across sessions.

How it works

From data input to meaningful energy behavior.

1. Define your home

Add appliances, assign wattages, and decide which devices are on so the app can model real usage.

2. Simulate active load

The prototype increases appliance runtime over time and calculates total energy based on active devices.

3. Respond to peak alerts

When a challenge begins, users reduce load to avoid peaks and earn points for successfully lowering usage.

4. Measure rewards

A built-in reward system tracks points, streaks, and badges to keep users motivated and engaged.

FAQ

Common questions answered

What does PowerPulse prototype demonstrate?

It showcases a mobile energy coaching flow with appliance tracking, peak event challenges, cost estimates, and reward mechanics.

Is this built as a real app?

Yes. It is a working Expo/React Native prototype that can be launched on mobile devices for demos and testing.

Can it simulate energy savings?

Absolutely. The app calculates usage and points based on appliance activity, making savings visible through simulated behavior.

Who benefits most from this prototype?

Energy teams, hackathon projects, and product designers who want a strong demo for home energy awareness.
